## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end cultural wedding and celebration venue experience for a prestigious banquet destination in Swat, Pakistan. The visual character merges aristocratic Mughal-Swati architectural romance with pristine contemporary hospitality standards. 

The aesthetic is built on **Regal Modernity**—a balanced union of deep, lush emerald jewel tones, radiant polished golds, and airy ivory architectural surfaces. The experience must evoke unhurried grandeur, generational pride, warmth, and peerless hospitality. It departs from clichéd wedding pastels by employing deep forest verdancy, crisp architectural geometry, fine metallic filaments, and dignified bilingual typographic poise.

## Colors

The palette balances monumental emerald depths with ceremonial warm metallics, anchored against warm architectural whites:

- **Primary Emerald (`#064E3B`)**: The soul of the design system. Represents timeless prestige, fertile Swat valleys, and regal velvet court settings. Used for primary buttons, deep hero backdrops, elevated headers, and impactful brand elements.
- **Deep Velvet Emerald (`#022C22`)**: An ultra-deep tone for high-contrast dark sections, evening banquet displays, and immersive footer treatments.
- **Imperial Gold (`#D97706`) & Warm Radiant Amber (`#F59E0B`, `#B45309`)**: Secondary accents used for micro-borders, metallic rim highlights, badges, active states, and decorative ornamental filigree.
- **Warm Gold Tint (`#FEF3C7`)**: Soft luminous highlight tone for subtle hover fills, champagne badges, and subtle border halos.
- **Alabaster & Ivory (`#FAF5ED`, `#FDFBF7`, `#FFFFFF`)**: The light canvas foundation. Avoid pure sterile blue-whites; surfaces must evoke polished Italian marble, ivory silk, and sunlit courtyard travertine.
- **Charcoal Slate (`#1E293B`, `#0F172A`)**: Ensures immaculate typographic contrast and legibility across light backgrounds without introducing harsh jet black.

## Typography

The type system pairs high-contrast transitional serif elegance with ultra-legible contemporary geometry. 

- **Display & Headings (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical royalty, timeless grandeur, and invitation-grade refinement. Display sizes employ deliberate negative tracking to maintain sharp focus. Italicized cuts should be reserved for poetic accents, signature quotes, and menu course headers.
- **Interface & Content (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers crisp readability across package pricing, event schedules, capacity numbers, and operational details.
- **Urdu/Nastaliq Integration**: Where Nastaliq script is displayed (such as the hall's honorific name and cultural welcome signatures), ensure vertical line-height breathing room is amplified by at least 1.4x relative to standard Latin characters to prevent diacritic clipping.
- **Labels & Metadata**: Set in uppercase with wide letter tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to reinforce bespoke luxury editorial cues.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an unhurried 12-column fluid grid system bounded by generous architectural margins:

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer canvas padding. Hero tiers and curated photo suites use asymmetric full-bleed or wide offset grids to replicate high-end lifestyle monographs.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Two-column booking and package comparisons stack progressively.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. All dense luxury grids seamlessly collapse to single vertical columns with horizontal snap-scroll showcases for banquet hall galleries.
- **Spacing Principle**: Generous whitespace signals opulence. Never crowd package tiers, catering menus, or bridal suite features. Use `space-xl` between modular sections and `space-lg` internally within structural cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth reflects palace architecture—carved archways, soft candlelight, and brushed brass fixtures rather than stark digital drops.

- **Tonal Layering**: Light themes use layered warm ivory surfaces (`#FAF5ED` base overlaid with `#FFFFFF` cards). Dark banquet/VIP themes employ `#022C22` base with `#064E3B` elevated containers.
- **Ambient Candlelit Shadows**: Elevation uses warm amber-tinged diffusion rather than cold grey shadows:
  - Low elevation (Cards, controls): `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(6, 78, 59, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.04)`.
  - High elevation (Modals, booking drawer, floating menu bar): `0 20px 45px -10px rgba(2, 44, 34, 0.18), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
- **Metallic Borders**: Surfaces are bordered by fine hairline strokes (`1px`) in gold tints (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)` or `linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.4), rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.1))`) to evoke inlaid gold leaf trim.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft Architectural (`1`)** shape language:

- **Baseline Geometry**: Standard containers, inputs, and cards use `0.25rem` (4px) rounded corners, ensuring a tailored, crisp, and aristocratic silhouette reminiscent of stonework and formal stationery.
- **Structural Accents (`rounded-lg` / 0.5rem & `rounded-xl` / 0.75rem)**: Reserved for hero image frames, featured bridal suite highlights, and modal dialogs.
- **Arched Motifs**: Feature headers and imagery showcases may incorporate top-only curved arch profiles (Mughal/Swati cusped arch geometry) to honor traditional craftsmanship without cluttering interactive UI components.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Imperial Button**: Rich Emerald (`#064E3B`) fill with crisp Ivory text (`#FDFBF7`), subtle top-edge 1px gold sheen, and `0.25rem` corners. On hover, background shifts to Deep Velvet Emerald (`#022C22`) accompanied by a subtle gold glow.
- **Royal Gold (Accent) Button**: Gradient fill from `#F59E0B` to `#D97706` with white or dark emerald text, designated exclusively for high-intent actions like "Check Date Availability" and "Schedule a Private Tour."
- **Secondary (Outline) Button**: Transparent fill, 1px perimeter border in `#D97706`, text in `#064E3B` (or `#FEF3C7` on dark sections), transitioning on hover to a delicate champagne fill (`rgba(254, 243, 197, 0.25)`).

### Cards & Hall Showcases
- White (`#FFFFFF`) canvas framed in a dual-line hairline border: outer boundary `1px solid rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.18)` and an inner inset margin of 4px with a 0.5px faint gold rule.
- Image aspect ratios fixed to 16:10 or 4:3 with warm golden hour color grading and gentle image zoom on card hover.

### Chips & Hospitality Badges
- Pill-form or subtle 4px tags with pale gold fill (`#FEF3C7`), thin amber border (`#F59E0B`), and `#B45309` typography. Used for capacity indicators (e.g., "1,200 Guests", "Segregated & Mixed Layouts", "VIP Bridal Suite Included").

### Form Fields & Inquiries
- Text inputs and date pickers use `#FFFFFF` background with an unyielding `#E2E8F0` border that activates to a 1.5px `#064E3B` contour with a subtle `#D97706` focus halo ring. 
- Input labels use `label-md` in uppercase Charcoal (`#1E293B`) paired with delicate placeholder hints.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes with 2px radius; checked state fills with `#064E3B` highlighting a pure gold (`#F59E0B`) checkmark.
- Radio buttons feature a concentric gold ring enclosing an emerald center dot.

### Specialized Venue Components
- **Cultural Schedule Timeline**: Vertical timeline in gold filigree connecting Nikah, Barat, and Walima stage milestones.
- **Bilingual Title Pairing**: Standardized component rendering the English name alongside an authentic Urdu Nastaliq calligraphic badge (`قصرِ نور`).
- **Menu Curation Drawer**: Accordion items detailed with fine gold dashed dividers and emerald item headers.
